## 🎯 Design Philosophy

### "Tell a Story, Not Just Numbers"

Rather than showing:
```
Healthy Percentage: 87%
Diseased Percentage: 13%
Total Detections: 5
Most Common: Powdery Mildew
```

We show:
```
✅ EXCELLENT HEALTH
Your crops are thriving! Keep up the good work.
Health Score: 87%

🦠 DISEASE THREATS
1. Powdery Mildew - 45% (Critical)
2. Leaf Spot - 20% (Medium)

💡 RECOMMENDATIONS
✅ Maintain current care practices
📋 Continue regular monitoring
🔍 Focus on Powdery Mildew treatment
```

This makes instant sense to a farmer. No interpretation needed.
