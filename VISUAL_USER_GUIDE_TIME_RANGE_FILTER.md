## 🖱️ How to Use the Filter

### Step 1: Open Statistics Page
```
Main Menu
  ↓
Tap "Statistics" icon
  ↓
Statistics page loads with default 7-day view
```

### Step 2: Choose Your Time Range

#### To View Last 7 Days:
```
1. Look for the 3 buttons near the top
2. Tap the "[📅 7 Days]" button
3. Button turns green
4. Chart shows last 7 days of data
5. Title changes to "Detection Activity (Last 7 Days)"
```

#### To View Last 30 Days:
```
1. Tap the "[📆 30 Days]" button
2. Button turns green
```

```
1. Tap the "[📅 All Time]" button
```

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Why are there only 3 options?
**A:** These three options (7 days, 30 days, all-time) cover 90% of user needs:
- Weekly monitoring (7 days)
- Monthly planning (30 days)
- Historical analysis (all-time)

### Q: What if I have less than 7 days of data?
**A:** The chart shows whatever data is available. If you've only been monitoring for 3 days, the 7-day view will show those 3 days. The message adjusts to tell you how many days of data you have.

### Q: Can I export the filtered data?
**A:** Yes! Look for the "Export" button at the bottom of the Statistics page. It will export whatever time range is currently selected.

### Q: Does the filter work on phones and tablets?
**A:** Yes! The buttons automatically adjust to fit your screen size. They work the same way on all devices.

### Q: How often is the data updated?
**A:** The data is updated based on your detection scans. Each time you scan a field, the timeline updates with the new detection.

### Q: Can I switch time ranges quickly?
**A:** Absolutely! Tap a different button and the chart updates instantly - no waiting, no page reload.

### Q: What do the bar heights mean?
**A:** Each bar represents one day. The height of the bar shows how many disease detections were recorded that day. Taller bar = more detections.

### Q: Why is there a message at the bottom of the chart?
**A:** The message explains what time range you're viewing and suggests what it's best used for. It helps you understand the context of the data.
