## 💡 WHAT IT DOES FOR FARMERS

When they tap the Statistics tab, they see:

1. **Summary Cards** - Quick numbers at a glance
   - Total detections
   - Number of unique diseases
   - Field health percentage
   - Disease percentage

2. **Health Meter** - Visual field condition
   - Green (70-100%): Excellent
   - Orange (40-70%): Needs attention
   - Red (<40%): Needs immediate care

3. **Pie Chart** - Disease distribution
   - Which diseases are most common
   - Percentage of each

4. **Timeline Chart** - 30-day trends
   - How detections change over time
   - Identify patterns

5. **Rankings Table** - Top diseases
   - Ranked by frequency
   - Count and percentage for each

6. **Action Buttons**
   - Export data as JSON
   - Clear history (with confirmation)
