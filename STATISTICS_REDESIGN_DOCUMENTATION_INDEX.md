## 🎨 Design Philosophy

**"Tell the Crop's Story"**

Transform raw data into a compelling narrative that helps farmers:
- **What** happened with their crops
- **Why** it matters
- **What** they should do about it

## 💡 Storytelling Techniques Used

1. **The Status Hook** - Immediate emotional response
2. **Context Building** - Answer "What data are we working with?"
3. **Problem Identification** - Highlight "What's wrong?"
4. **Trend Analysis** - Show "Is it getting better?"
5. **Solution Offering** - Guide "What should I do?"
6. **Confidence Building** - Reassure "How reliable is this?"
7. **Call-to-Action** - Enable "What's next?"
