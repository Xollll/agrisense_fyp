## 🎨 Technical Specifications

### Icon Design
- **Type**: Custom painted neural network
- **Nodes**: 5 arranged in pentagon
- **Center**: 1 pulsing white hub
- **Connections**: Animated lines between all nodes
- **Colors**: Cyan → Purple gradient
- **Effects**: Individual glow halos, moving particles

### Animation System
| Animation | Duration | Cycle | Effect |
|-----------|----------|-------|--------|
| Menu open/close | 500ms | Once | Smooth transition |
| Icon rotation | 500ms | Once | Synced with menu |
| Pulse | 1500ms | Infinite | Breathing effect |
| Bounce | 900ms | Infinite | Vertical bob (when menu closed) |
| Particle burst | 500ms | Once | Expansion + fade |
| Moving dots | Continuous | Loop | Travel on lines |
| Line glow | 1500ms | Infinite | Opacity pulse |

### Glow System
- **Layer 1**: Cyan outer (60% opacity, 35px blur) - Neon effect
- **Layer 2**: Purple middle (40% opacity, 25px blur) - Color harmony
- **Layer 3**: Blue inner (30% opacity, 15px blur) - Cohesion
- **Layer 4**: Deep shadow (25% opacity, 12px offset) - Grounding

### Performance
- **Frame Rate**: 60 FPS (maintained throughout)
- **Memory Overhead**: ~2KB (negligible)
- **CPU Usage**: Low-Medium (efficient)
- **GPU Usage**: Low (minimal)
- **No breaking changes**: 100% compatible
